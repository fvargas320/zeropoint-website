How it works

Three steps from bay to calendar.

Log the session, tag range and gear, then open the calendar anytime — inventory and alerts stay with you.

Log

Session logging that stays organized.

Capture the range day once — range, friends, guns, ammo, and notes — then find it again on the calendar.

  • Sessions with range, friends, guns, and ammo
  • Armory & calibers so every firearm stays labeled
  • Ammo inventory by brand and grain
  • Drills — Dot Torture, Bill Drill, and custom

Inventory & alerts

Guns, ammo, and maintenance in one place.

Track what you own and what you used. Get low-ammo alerts and cleaning reminders based on rounds logged.

  • Gun & ammo inventory by brand and grain
  • Low-ammo alerts before the next range day
  • Maintenance alerts by rounds fired — you set the interval
  • Calendar & weekly digest of sessions and rounds

Train with others

Tag buddies and share results.

Keep a list of who you shoot with, join crews, and share a session or drill card when you want to.

  • Shooting buddies tagged on sessions
  • Crews & leaderboards for the group that trains together
  • One-tap share cards for a session or drill result

Training rhythm

Calendar and weekly digest.

See which days you made it to the range, then get a short weekly summary of sessions and rounds — no digging through notes.

  • Range-day heatmap Which days you logged a session, at a glance
  • Weekly digest Rounds fired, sessions logged, and trend direction
  • Consistency check Notice quiet weeks so you can plan the next range day
