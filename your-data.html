Your data

Save your photos and videos

Your range records stay in ZeroPoint. Cloud copies of photos and videos are removed 12 months after Pro ends. Saving them is free.

Open ZeroPoint

Two free steps in the app

  1. Open Settings → Your data → Restore from cloud backup. This brings your photos and videos back onto your phone.
  2. Then tap Export my data to save everything, media included, wherever you keep files.

Prefer to keep them in the cloud? Resubscribe to ZeroPoint Pro in the app and nothing is removed.

Don't have the app on this phone?

Install ZeroPoint and sign in with the same account, then follow the steps above.
