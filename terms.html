Description of Service

ZeroPoint is a session-logging and inventory application for shooting ranges. It lets you:

  • Log shooting range sessions
  • Tag ranges, friends, guns, and ammo
  • Track firearm and ammunition inventory
  • Receive low-ammo and maintenance alerts based on rounds logged
  • View sessions on a calendar and review weekly digests
  • Attach photos and notes to sessions
  • Manage shooting buddies, crews, and drills

ZeroPoint does not detect shots automatically or measure paper targets with computer vision. Session data is entered by you.
